# Brand Safety

## Accuracy

When editing a real __PROJECT_NAME__ building image:

- Preserve the building.
- Preserve architecture, proportions, and facade details.
- Avoid adding misleading construction progress or site conditions.

## Branding

Use __PROJECT_NAME__ branding only when supplied or explicitly requested. If a logo must appear on an object, such as a hard hat, use the approved logo asset when available.

## Avoid

- Fake contact details
- Misleading signage
- Unrealistic architectural changes
- Off-brand novelty
- Crowds or people unless requested
- Text baked into images unless approved
