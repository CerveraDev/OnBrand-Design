## How To Use

For each cluster, fill `Decision` with one or more roles, or `none`. Then set `Rights confirmed`.

| Role | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `social-post` | Usable as-is for a single static social post |
| `social-carousel` | Usable as a carousel slide |
| `social-crop-source` | Approved to be cropped/reframed into a social format (see `LIM-022`) |
| `none` | Not approved for social |

`Proposed` is a recommendation based on orientation and subject, **not an approval**. Landscape assets default to `social-crop-source` because they cannot fill a square or vertical format without an approved crop. Use `Exceptions` to override individual files within a cluster.

Rights are a separate question from suitability: a photo licensed for email and web is not automatically licensed for organic or paid social.
