# Phase 3: Campaign Strategy And Copy System

**Status:** Planned  
**Target:** 0.4.0  
**Depends on:** Phases 1 and 2 for final acceptance

## Goal

Produce project-specific campaign strategy and complete email copy from either a detailed brief or a broad campaign idea.

## Requirements

- COPY-001: Classify directed-build versus concept-development mode.
- COPY-002: Ask only for missing information that materially affects output.
- COPY-003: Generate campaign angle, subject line, preview text, headline, optional subheading, body copy, and CTA.
- COPY-004: Support invitations, recaps, construction updates, amenity spotlights, residence/floorplan spotlights, broker outreach, appointments, and nurture.
- COPY-005: Avoid unsupported pricing, availability, dates, contact information, and claims.
- COPY-006: Tie visual direction and module selection to the campaign objective.
- COPY-007: Require user selection before advancing from concept options to final assembly.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Directed briefs proceed without unnecessary discovery questions.
- Broad briefs return clearly differentiated, usable options.
- Copy matches the selected project's calibrated voice and available factual inputs.
- CTA language remains consistent across subject, body, buttons, and variants.
