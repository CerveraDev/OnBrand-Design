# Brand Safety

## Accuracy

When editing a real Rider building image:

- Preserve the building.
- Preserve architecture, proportions, and facade details.
- Avoid adding misleading construction progress or site conditions.
- Preserve real Rider amenity and interior context when the prompt names a Rider gym, lobby, arrival, exterior, or other project space.

## Branding

Use Rider branding only when supplied or explicitly requested. If a logo must appear on an object, such as a hard hat, use the approved logo asset when available.

Record logo references as manifest assets or checksummed scaffold/local paths in the final provenance. Do not invent marks.

## Avoid

- Fake contact details
- Misleading signage
- Unrealistic architectural changes
- Off-brand novelty
- Crowds or people unless requested
- Text baked into images unless approved
