# Campaign Types

This list is provisional and should be refined with real Cassia campaigns.

## Core Types

- Pre-event invitation
- Post-event recap
- Construction update
- Sales momentum update
- Amenity spotlight
- Residence or floorplan spotlight
- Broker outreach
- Private appointment push
- General brand/nurture email

## Campaign-Type Behavior

Pre-event emails should create interest, urgency, and attendance intent.

Post-event emails should document momentum, turnout, progress, and social proof.

Construction updates should communicate progress and confidence without overexplaining technical details.

Amenity and residence spotlights should focus on desirability, lifestyle, and specific differentiators.

Broker outreach should make it easy for brokers to understand the opportunity and forward or adapt the piece.
